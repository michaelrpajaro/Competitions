THE BIG PICTURE

How It All Fits Together

There are two tracks running at once — a free ladder that anyone can jump into, and a set of separate paid/seasonal or prize-driven programs alongside it. Here's how they relate.

1 · Free Ladder
Weekly Qualifiers, Monthly Challenges, and Thursday Sprint Races — open to everyone, running continuously. Top finishers earn an invite.
→
2 · Monthly Championship
Invite-only race held at month's end. Beat the field — and outpace the SIM1Racing team drivers — to prove you belong at the top.
→
3 · Join the Team
Win the championship and you're offered a spot on the SIM1Racing team — free 24/7 access and a seat in national & international competition.
🏆
Leagues
Separate, paid multi-week seasons (Audi TT Cup, custom club leagues, the Lotus GTC league) with their own standings and prize pools — parallel to the free ladder above.
🚗
Giveaways & Events
Real-world prizes layered on top of racing — a car giveaway, a monthly endurance race, and a free track day — each with its own rules and entry window.
🛠️
Custom Leagues
Not a competition you enter — a service you book. Bring your own group, pick the car and track, and we run the season for you.
