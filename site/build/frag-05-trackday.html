How It Works

One track, three weeks to set your best lap. Jump on the SIM1Racing server any time the competition is open, post a clean time, and the top two when it closes win a real track day — for free.

01
Join the Competition
Come into SIM1, head to the multiplayer section, and join the competition. To compete, purchase any daily rate and you're in — with as many laps as you want for the day. Gold members can drive unlimited laps for a chance at winning.
02
Keep Improving
The same track stays open for three weeks. Come back as many times as you want to chase a faster time — only your quickest clean lap counts toward the standings.
03
Top Two Win
When the window closes, 1st place takes the two-day weekend pass ($900 value) and 2nd place wins a single-day ticket ($500 value) to Lime Rock Park June 19–20.
📌 Important — Event Date

The track day is June 19–20, 2026 at Lime Rock Park, CT. Winners must be able to attend. The prize is a ticket — all other costs (travel, lodging, optional car rental) are the winner's responsibility. Prizes are non-transferable and have no cash value.
