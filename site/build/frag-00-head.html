THE BIG PICTURE

How It All Fits Together

There are two tracks running at once — open competitions anyone can jump into, and a set of separate paid/seasonal or prize-driven programs alongside it.

1 · Open Competitions
Weekly Qualifiers, Monthly Challenges, and Thursday Sprint Races — open to everyone, running continuously. Top finishers earn an invite.
→
2 · Monthly Championship
Invite-only race held at month's end. Beat the field — and outpace the SIM1Racing team drivers — to prove you belong at the top.
→
3 · Join the Team
Win the championship and you're offered a spot on the SIM1Racing team — free 24/7 access and a seat in national & international competition.
🏆
Leagues
A new Monthly League every month — paid multi-week seasons with their own standings and prize pools — or book a fully custom season for your own group.
🚗
Giveaways
Car giveaways, free track days, and more.

Endurance Race
Also a month-to-month thing — just like Leagues gets a new season every month, the Endurance Race runs once a month too. Solo or with a co-driver.
