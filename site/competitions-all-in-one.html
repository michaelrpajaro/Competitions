How the Competition Works

Weekly Qualifier
Post your fastest lap in this week's Car of the Week. Runs Monday–Sunday. Top 3 earn a championship invite.
⏱️
Monthly Challenge
Month-long time attack open to all. Top 3 earn a championship invite.
🏁
Thursday Sprint
Live race every Thursday 8PM. 1 invite per race — goes to the highest podium finisher not already qualified.
🏎️ Car of the Week

The Weekly Qualifier and the Thursday Sprint Race share the same car and track each week — the Car of the Week. Race it solo, on your own time, in the Weekly Qualifier — or show up live Thursday at 8PM and race it wheel-to-wheel against the field in the Sprint. Same combo, your choice of format.

▼
🏆
Monthly Championship Race
Qualified drivers only. Held end of every month. 10 min practice → 20 min qualifying → 30 min race. Only the best earn their way here.

Join the SIM1Racing Team
Win the championship and outpace our SIM1 team drivers to earn your invite — free 24/7 access, unlimited sim time, and national & international competition.

* Championship qualification resets each month. Weekly & monthly: 3 invites per event — awarded to the highest 3 finishers who aren't staff and aren't already qualified (later positions can bump up). Sprint: 1 invite per race, awarded to the highest podium (top 3) finisher who isn't staff and isn't already qualified; if all three are ineligible, no spot is given that week. Special events: one-day events with their own prizes — they do not contribute to championship qualification.

THE FORMAT

How Each Giveaway Works

Every giveaway runs the same way — the rules and prize ladder don't change, just the car. Four weeks, a fresh sim track each week, top finishers earn entries.

⚡ Repeat Winners — Why Some Weeks Pay More Than 15

Every week, 15 unique winners fill the 15 prize slots at full value. But you can win in more than one week — and repeat wins don't take a slot from a first-timer, they pay on top. So a week can pay more than 15 people.

Example — in Week 2 the fastest driver already won in Week 1. They collect 50% of 1st place = 5,000. The 15 prize slots then go to the next 15 first-time winners, so the Week-2 runner-up (a first-timer) takes the full 10,000 first-place prize. That week pays 16 people.

ENTRIES ON THE LINE

Weekly Prize Ladder

Finish in the top 15 each week to earn entries. Prizes are awarded in entries — the giveaway's currency.
